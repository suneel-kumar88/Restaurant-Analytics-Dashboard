# Restaurant Analytics Dashboard

An interactive Power BI dashboard designed to track sales performance, profit trends, menu popularity, staff contribution, and customer dining experiences across multiple global cities.

---

## 📌 Dashboard Overview

![Restaurant Analytics Dashboard] https://github.com/suneel-kumar88/Restaurant-Analytics-Dashboard/blob/c767eb0dddefa45b91b763234b0715915a3e6773/Restaurent%20Dashboard.png
 
This dashboard gives restaurant managers, food & beverage executives, and business analysts clear visibility into revenue drivers, customer behavior, ordering channels, and service ratings to make data-backed operational decisions.

---

* **Total Sales:** 30K
* **Total Orders:** 489 orders
* **Total Profit:** 15K
* **Total Customers:** 179 unique customers
* **Average Order Value (AOV):** 61.83
* **Average Ranking / Rating:** 3.99 / 5.0

## 📊 Visual Breakdown & Insights

### 1. Revenue & Profit Trends
* **Monthly Sales Trend:** Consistent upward growth peaking towards the end of the year, starting from 1.0K (Jan) up to 3.8K (Oct & Dec).
* **Monthly Profit Trend:** Steady margin scaling from 0.5K in January up to 1.9K in October and December.

### 2. Geographical & Staff Performance
* **City-Wise Sales:**
  * **Paris:** 10K
  * **Tokyo:** 7K
  * **Singapore:** 6K
  * **Dubai:** 5K
  * **Toronto:** 2K
  * **Sydney:** 1K
* **Top 5 Waiters by Sales:** William (6.3K), Lucas (6.2K), Noah (6.0K), Oliver (5.9K), and Ethan (5.8K).

### 3. Menu & Category Analysis
* **Top 5 Selling Menu Items:**
  * **Tiramisu:** 158 units
  * **BBQ Chicken Burger:** 139 units
  * **Grilled Salmon:** 128 units
  * **Ribeye Steak:** 123 units
  * **Lasagna:** 115 units
* **Sales by Food Category:** Burgers (19%), Desserts (19%), Steak (19%), Seafood (18%), Pasta (15%), and Pizza (10%).
* **Average Rating by Category:** Burgers lead customer satisfaction at 4.2, followed by Pizza (4.0), Seafood (4.0), Steak (4.0), Pasta (3.9), and Desserts (3.9).

### 4. Operations & Customer Distribution
* **Dining Type Distribution:** Takeaway leads at 38%, Delivery at 35%, and Dine-In accounts for 27%.
* **Payment Method Breakdown:** Card (29%), Cash (27%), UPI (27%), and Wallet (18%).
* **Customer Type Distribution:** Occasional visitors make up 55%, New customers represent 40%, and Regulars represent 4%.

---

## 🎛️ Interactive Filters & Slicers

* **City:** Filter across Paris, Tokyo, Singapore, Dubai, Toronto, and Sydney.
* **Dining Type:** Analyze individual fulfillment channels (Dine-In, Delivery, Takeaway).
* **Month:** Time-based filtering across operational months.
* **Menu Item:** Granular performance tracking by individual menu item.
* **Date Slicer:** Multi-period calendar filter.

---

## 🛠️ Built With

* **BI Tool:** Microsoft Power BI Desktop
* **Data Modeling:** Power Query 
* **Calculations:** DAX (Measures for Total Sales, Margin %, Average Order Value, and Customer Ratings)
* **UI/UX Design:** Dark modern dashboard theme with high-contrast KPI cards and structured donut/bar visuals
